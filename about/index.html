About Total Dynamic Services

Since 2010, TDS has grown from a single snow plow into a full-service commercial property contractor, trusted by strata, municipal, institutional and commercial clients across Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley.

Get a free quote Meet the team

Our story

From one snow plow to a full-service property contractor

Total Dynamic Services began in 2010 as a snow removal contractor serving properties across Metro Vancouver. As clients came to rely on our consistency, they began asking us to support more of their year-round property needs.

We responded by investing in our people, equipment and capabilities, and by growing deliberately. That steady approach let us expand into landscape maintenance and construction, environmental services, site repairs, vegetation management and civil support work.

Today we are a commercial landscaping and property maintenance partner for commercial, strata, municipal and institutional clients throughout the Lower Mainland, backed by a company-owned fleet, experienced field teams and long-standing client relationships built on reliable delivery, season after season.

Common questions

Quick answers about working with TDS

Does TDS use subcontractors?

No. Work is delivered by our own trained crews using company-owned equipment, so one accountable team handles your site from start to finish.

What types of properties does TDS work on?

We support commercial, strata, municipal, institutional and industrial properties across Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley.

Is TDS safety certified?

TDS is a WorkSafeBC registered contractor in good standing with SafeContractor, Safe Company and BCCSA COR credentials. Read more on our safety page.
