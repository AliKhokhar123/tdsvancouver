Overview

Site Repairs & Waterproofing from one accountable team

We help commercial, strata, institutional, municipal and public-sector properties address site deficiencies before they become larger operational issues, safety risks or capital costs. Our work is focused on protecting the condition, function and long-term performance of the site.

All work is completed by in-house crews using company-owned equipment. We regularly work in active environments, including healthcare campuses, occupied strata properties and operating industrial and municipal sites, with a strong focus on minimizing disruption to tenants, staff and the public.

FAQ

Questions about site repairs & waterproofing

What site repair and waterproofing services does TDS provide?

TDS provides commercial site repair and waterproofing, including concrete repairs, asphalt patching, pothole repairs, drainage correction, paver repairs, accessibility improvements, pressure washing and waterproofing systems for parkades, rooftops and below-grade structures.

Does TDS provide waterproofing for parkades and below-grade structures?

Yes. Services include waterproofing membranes, protective coatings, epoxy flooring and caulking systems designed to help prevent moisture intrusion and protect surfaces from long-term deterioration.

Can TDS repair drainage problems and catch basins?

Yes. TDS provides catch basin and perimeter drain inspection, cleaning, repair and rehabilitation to restore proper drainage, reduce standing water and surface flooding, and protect surrounding infrastructure.

Which areas does TDS serve for site repairs and waterproofing?

We serve commercial, strata, institutional, municipal and public-sector properties across Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley.
