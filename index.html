FAQ

Common questions from property managers

What types of properties does TDS work on?

We serve commercial, strata, industrial, institutional and municipal properties across Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley, from single sites to larger multi-service contracts.

Are your crews employees or subcontractors?

Our work is delivered in-house by trained TDS crews using company-owned equipment, so one team is accountable for your site.

How fast can you respond to a snow event?

Our direct management structure lets us mobilize quickly during seasonal peaks and emergency callouts, and our snow and ice crews operate around the clock during winter weather.

How do I know the work was done?

For snow and ice management we document each visit in real time, including service times, site photos, weather observations and GPS-verified attendance records.

Is TDS certified and safety compliant?

Yes. Crews are trained, uniformed and WorkSafeBC covered, and TDS holds recognized safety and industry credentials including SafeContractor, Safe Company, BCCSA COR, VRCA and SIMA membership, plus GHG Protocol Scope 1 and 2 certification.

Looking for a property services partner you can count on?

TDS provides tailored, in-house property solutions for property managers, corporate facilities, stratas, developers and general contractors across Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley.
