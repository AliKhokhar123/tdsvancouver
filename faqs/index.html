About TDS

What types of properties does TDS work on?

We support commercial, strata, municipal, institutional and industrial properties across Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley, from single-site maintenance to multi-service contracts.

Does TDS use subcontractors?

No. Work is delivered by our own trained crews using company-owned equipment, so one accountable team handles your site from start to finish.

Which areas does TDS serve?

Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley, with active contracts in Surrey, Langley, Abbotsford, Delta, Richmond, Burnaby, Vancouver and surrounding municipalities.

Is TDS certified and insured?

TDS is a WorkSafeBC registered contractor in good standing with SafeContractor, Safe Company and BCCSA COR credentials, and is a member of VRCA and SIMA. We carry commercial liability and property insurance.

How do I get a quote?

Use the form on our contact page, email sales@tdsvancouver.com or call 604-210-6629. Tell us about the property, the services you need and the city, and we will follow up.

Does TDS offer carbon neutral services?

Yes. TDS tracks fuel consumption across its fleet and purchases verified carbon credits to offset the emissions from its services. This supports clients with environmental and ESG objectives.

Commercial landscaping

What is included in commercial landscaping?

Commercial landscaping can include lawn care, pruning, hedge trimming, planting, garden bed maintenance, irrigation monitoring, seasonal cleanups, vegetation management and snow and ice management. The scope depends on the property, the contract and the season.

Does TDS provide landscaping for office parks and business campuses?

Yes. We look after office parks and multi-building campuses across Metro Vancouver, including entrances, courtyards and perimeter cutbacks, under a single schedule. One provider means fewer service gaps and less back-and-forth between vendors.

Does TDS serve retail centres and shopping plazas?

Yes. We support retail plazas, shopping centres, business parks, office properties and mixed-use developments with in-house crews and company-owned equipment.

What should I look for in a commercial landscaping contract?

A clear scope of work, service frequency, seasonal maintenance plan, irrigation responsibilities, snow and ice details, insurance coverage, communication process, reporting expectations and sustainability practices. A well-defined contract avoids scope disputes later.

Does commercial landscaping include irrigation management?

It can. Irrigation work may include spring activation, monitoring, seasonal adjustments, leak identification and winter blowouts. Confirm whether irrigation is part of the contract or a separate scope, because it affects budgeting and plant survival.

Snow and ice management

What snow and ice management services does TDS provide?

Snow plowing, salting and de-icing, sidewalk clearing, pedestrian access maintenance and site monitoring, to keep parking areas, entrances, access roads and walkways safer and accessible in winter weather.

Does TDS provide snow removal for commercial and strata properties?

Yes. We serve commercial, strata, industrial, institutional and municipal properties. Company-owned equipment and in-house crews support winter service tailored to each property's size, access needs and operations.

How does TDS help prevent ice on walkways and parking areas?

We use targeted salting and de-icing with calibrated spreaders and application rates suited to the surface and weather, plus mechanical and manual clearing of sidewalks, entrances, stairs, ramps and accessible routes.

How is snow service documented?

Each visit is documented in real time, including service times, site photos, weather observations and GPS-verified attendance records, so you have clear proof of service.

Can landscaping and snow removal be combined in one contract?

Yes. Many commercial contracts combine year-round grounds care with winter services such as snow removal, de-icing, walkway clearing and parking access support. TDS runs both with the same in-house crews and equipment.

Site repairs and waterproofing

What site repair and waterproofing services does TDS provide?

Concrete repairs, asphalt patching, pothole repairs, drainage correction, paver repairs, accessibility improvements, pressure washing and waterproofing systems for parkades, rooftops and below-grade structures.

Does TDS provide waterproofing for parkades and below-grade structures?

Yes. Services include waterproofing membranes, protective coatings, epoxy flooring and caulking systems designed to help prevent moisture intrusion and long-term deterioration.

Can TDS repair drainage problems and catch basins?

Yes. We inspect, clean, repair and rehabilitate catch basins and perimeter drains to restore proper drainage, reduce standing water and surface flooding, and protect surrounding infrastructure.

Sensitive habitat and environmental

What environmental services does TDS provide?

Invasive species management, herbicide application, native planting and habitat restoration, tree watering and care, erosion and sediment control, and vegetation management in sensitive sites.

Which invasive plants does TDS manage?

We provide manual and chemical control of priority invasive plants such as Japanese knotweed, Himalayan blackberry, giant hogweed and poison hemlock, with application records and compliance documentation.

Is herbicide applied by licensed professionals?

Programs are led by personnel holding current BC Pesticide Applicator Licences and include pre-treatment assessments, application records, post-treatment signage, monitoring and equipment decontamination between sites.

Can TDS work in environmentally sensitive areas?

Yes. We carry out vegetation cutback, flail mowing, dyke mowing and clearing in areas with ecological sensitivity, restricted access or wildlife requirements, under site-specific environmental protocols.

Civil and excavation

What civil excavation services does TDS provide?

Site clearing, trenching, soil stabilization, backfilling, grading and road preparation, which prepare sites for construction, infrastructure installation and development.

Does TDS install drainage and utilities?

Yes. Our civil services include utility installation, drainage systems, stormwater management and water and wastewater infrastructure support, planned around site conditions and applicable standards.

Does TDS offer asphalt and concrete work?

Yes. We handle surface preparation, removal, installation, repairs and finishing for commercial properties, access roads and parking areas.
